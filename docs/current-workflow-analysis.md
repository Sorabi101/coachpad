# Current Workflow Analysis

## Purpose

This document describes how tennis coaches currently organize their daily work.

The goal is to understand the existing workflow, identify inefficiencies, and document recurring pain points before defining software requirements.

---

# Daily Workflow of a Tennis Coach

## 1. Scheduling Training Sessions

### Current Workflow

1. Player contacts the coach (usually via WhatsApp).
2. Coach checks personal availability.
3. Coach checks court availability.
4. Coach suggests one or more time slots.
5. Player confirms or rejects.
6. Coach books the court.
7. Coach creates a calendar event manually.

### Current Tools

- WhatsApp
- Google Calendar
- Club booking system
- Phone calls

### Pain Points

- Multiple messages are required to find a suitable appointment.
- Double bookings may occur.
- Free time slots are difficult to manage.
- Court reservations are separated from player communication.

---

# 2. Preparing a Training Session

### Current Workflow

1. Coach thinks about today's objective.
2. Coach searches for suitable exercises.
3. Coach creates a training plan (or improvises).

### Current Tools

- Notion
- YouTube
- Personal notes
- Memory

### Pain Points

- Exercises have to be searched repeatedly.
- Previous training plans are difficult to reuse.
- Planning requires unnecessary time.
- Many coaches skip planning completely.

---

# 3. Conducting the Training

### Current Workflow

1. Coach arrives at the court.
2. Training is conducted.
3. Coach gives verbal feedback.
4. Training ends.

### Current Tools

- Tennis equipment
- Personal experience

### Pain Points

- Feedback is often forgotten afterwards.
- No structured documentation exists.
- Progress is remembered rather than documented.
- Progress gets unnoticed by the player.

---

# 4. Documenting Player Progress

### Current Workflow

After training, the coach may:

- write notes in Notion (never happens)
- write notes on paper (never ever)
- remember everything 
- do nothing (Always!!!) Because it's just too much effort. Since the student doesn't know anything.

### Current Tools

- Notion
- Paper
- Apple Notes

### Pain Points

- Notes are inconsistent.
- Information is spread across different platforms.
- Previous notes are difficult to find.
- Players cannot access their progress.

---

# 5. Communication

### Current Workflow

Communication takes place before and after training.

Typical topics:

- scheduling
- cancellations
- payments
- questions
- training feedback

### Current Tools

- WhatsApp
- Phone
- SMS

### Pain Points

- Important information gets lost in chat history.
- Communication is not linked to the player profile.
- The same questions are answered repeatedly.

---

# 6. Payment

### Current Workflow

Players usually pay

- after each lesson
- by bank transfer
- via PayPal
- using a prepaid lesson package

### Current Tools

- PayPal
- Cash
- Online Banking

### Pain Points

- No payment history.
- Open lessons are difficult to track.
- Misunderstandings occur.

---

# Summary

The current workflow is highly fragmented.

A typical tennis coach switches between several different tools during a single working day.

| Activity | Current Tool |
|----------|--------------|
| Communication | WhatsApp |
| Scheduling | Google Calendar |
| Court Booking | Club System |
| Notes | Notion / Paper |
| Payments | PayPal / Cash |

As a result:

- Information is scattered across multiple applications.
- Administrative work consumes valuable coaching time.
- Player development is poorly documented.
- Repetitive manual work reduces efficiency.

These findings will serve as the foundation for defining the MVP and future user stories.
